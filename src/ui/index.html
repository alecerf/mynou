            Your space

            Just the way you like it.

            The essential settings, in one place.

            VPN

            Choose what goes through your WireGuard tunnel. With the VPN off, everything uses your own connection. Plex and this page never go through it.

            Friends

            Your Plex friends’ watchlists, read with your own Plex connection: they have nothing to set up. Turn a friend on and the films and series they add become requests, like yours.

            Network access

            Use Mynou from other computers on your local network. Other computers sign in with a password, unless the local network needs none; this Mac never needs to.
