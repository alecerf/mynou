## iCalendar export withdrawn

The private iCalendar (`.ics`) download, which never reached a release, is
withdrawn. Calendar views and scheduling are unchanged: the Calendar page,
`mynou calendar` and `GET /api/calendar` still list known episode dates, and
monitored series still acquire newly aired episodes. The former download address
now answers `410 Gone` without a file. See
[series and calendar](../../series.md#api-and-calendar).
