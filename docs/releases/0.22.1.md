# Mynou 0.22.1 — Original NNTP transport and reviewed connection probes

Native NNTP supports verified implicit TLS, strict greeting/AUTHINFO responses
and explicit BODY reads. Exact echoed article identities, bounded CRLF lines,
dot-unstuffing, absolute budgets and one active operation per provider reject
wrong, truncated, oversized or concurrent work. Authentication failures never
fall back. Provider errors and credentials stay out of reports and diagnostics.

Opt-in immutable provider settings and guarded CLI/API/browser probes check one
connection, authentication and QUIT without requesting articles or queuing media.
Guards bind settings, service identity and attempt counters; browser reviews also
bind the session and expire. Transport health is shared and ephemeral.
The yEnc encoded-input bound accounts for worst-case escape/line overhead while
retaining the original decoded-part and in-memory assembly limits.

Original local protocol and verified TLS fixtures require this source's complete
Actions run. Rust std only, zero dependencies and CI-only publication remain
mandatory. Disk-backed acquisition/recovery and Newznab follow in further 0.22
increments; archive extraction and PAR2 repair remain outside this contract.
